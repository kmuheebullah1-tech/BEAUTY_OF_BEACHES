Zone East Coastal Guide

Explore the East Beaches

Encounter emerald karst cliff lagoons, tropical rainforest shores, endless unbroken sands along the Bay of Bengal, and traditional paraw sailboat sunsets.

Karst Cliffs & Warm Waters

Archipelagos, Verdant Rainforests & Endless Shores

The eastern coastal zone spans from the Indian Ocean and Bay of Bengal across Southeast Asia to Polynesian atolls. Here you'll find Cox's Bazar, the world’s longest natural sandy beach (120 km), the dramatic limestone karst cliffs of Thailand’s Maya Bay, the virgin rainforest borders of Havelock Island in Andaman, and the legendary flour-like white sands of Boracay.

Travel Advisory

Eastern Coastal Travel Tips & Activities

Essential advice for dry season timing, plastic bans, and reef-safe tourism across eastern seas.

Dry Season (Nov–Apr)

For Andaman, Thailand, and Philippines, November to April offers crystal calm waters, low humidity, and prime snorkeling visibility.

Paraw Sunset Charters

At Boracay, traditional twin-outrigger sailboats catch evening breezes along Station 1 for one of Asia's most iconic sunsets.

Eco Bans on Plastics

Destinations like Boracay and Maya Bay enforce strict bans on single-use plastics and smoking directly on the beach. Carry a reusable flask.

Coral Nursery Respect

In revived eco-parks like Maya Bay, wading is restricted to designated boardwalk paths to foster blacktip reef shark reproduction.
