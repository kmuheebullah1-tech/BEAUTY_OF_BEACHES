Explore the North Beaches

From the bustling northern Arabian coastline of Manora in Pakistan to dramatic volcanic sea stacks in Iceland, mist-kissed Pacific shores, and Arctic fjords.

Geographic Character

Dramatic Cliffs, Sea Stacks & Northern Serenity

Northern beaches encompass diverse landscapes: from the tranquil warm sands and heritage lighthouses of Manora Peninsula in the northern Arabian Sea belt, to the awe-inspiring basalt columns of Iceland, midnight sun fjords in Norway, and ancient volcanic sea stacks of Northern Ireland. Each offers an unforgettable connection to the raw power of the ocean.

Travel Advisory

Northern Coastal Travel Tips & Activities

Practical recommendations to maximize your safety, comfort, and photography across northern coastal destinations.

Layered Windproof Wear

Northern marine climates can change rapidly. Always carry windbreakers and waterproof layers even in summer months.

Respect Sneaker Waves

At Atlantic shores like Reynisfjara, never turn your back on the surf. Sneaker waves can surge surprisingly high up the beach.

Tidal Awareness

Check local high and low tide timetables before exploring sea stacks or tidal pool caves at Cannon Beach or Ruby Beach.

Midnight Sun Golden Hours

In Arctic regions like Lofoten during June and July, the sun stays above the horizon, allowing 24-hour sunset photography.
