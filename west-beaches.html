Discover the West Beaches

Journey to historic Mediterranean shipwrecks, golden sandstone sea arches of Portugal's Algarve, cliffside Mayan ruins in Mexico, and tranquil Caribbean coral walls.

Ancient Coves & Atlantic Cliffs

Historic Shipwrecks, Golden Arches & Caribbean Calm

The western coastal zone brings together ancient history and dramatic seaside architecture. Experience Navagio Beach on Greece’s Ionian isle with its rusted 1980 shipwreck amphitheater, kayak through golden sea caves along Portugal's Praia da Marinha, swim below ancient 13th-century Mayan citadels at Tulum, and stroll twelve unbroken miles of sugar sand at Grace Bay.

Travel Advisory

Western Coastal Travel Tips & Activities

Crucial advice for boat charter safety, cliff base precautions, and seasonal sea cave kayaking.

Navagio Sea Conditions

Shipwreck Cove in Zakynthos is accessible only by boat. Always check morning swell reports before booking departure from Porto Vromi.

Sandstone Cliff Distancing

At Algarve beaches like Praia da Marinha, never spread towels directly beneath limestone overhangs due to occasional loose shale slides.

Mayan Ruins Early Visits

Visit Tulum Beach at 8:00 AM when gates open to capture El Castillo cliff fortress without crowds and swim in crystal Caribbean surf.

Barrier Reef Protection

At Grace Bay and Grand Cayman, protective barrier reefs keep nearshore swimming pool calm. Use biodegradable reef-safe sun protection.
