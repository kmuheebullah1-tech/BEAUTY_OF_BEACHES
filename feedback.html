Traveler Standards

How We Assess Coastal Quality

Community reviews evaluate shores across five core pillars to help world wanderers choose the best beach for their specific needs:

Cleanliness & Litter-Free Sands
Absence of plastic pollution and presence of trash receptacles.
Water Transparency
Visibility for snorkeling, swimming depth, and absence of strong undertows.
Safety & Lifeguards
Availability of posted flag warnings and clear swimming perimeter zones.
Sunset & Natural Ambiance
Scenic vistas, coastal breezes, and uncrowded evening tranquility.
